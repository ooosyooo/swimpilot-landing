Delete Your Account and Data

You can request deletion of your EopuEopu account and related data using the methods below.

1. How to Request Deletion

  • In the app: Open the app and go to Settings → Account → Delete Account. The request is processed immediately.
  • By email: If you cannot use the app, send a deletion request from the email address of the account you signed up with to o3orbit.labs@gmail.com. We verify you by the sending address and process it without delay.

Your account and data cannot be recovered after deletion. If you have an active subscription, cancel it yourself in the respective store (App Store / Google Play) to avoid further charges.

2. What Is Deleted

  • Account information (email, unique identifier)
  • Swim workout records (distance, duration, laps, heart rate, stroke, calories)
  • Profile information (nickname, country code, level, swimmer class)
  • Ranking statistics and leaderboard presence
  • Notification preferences and push tokens
  • Local records and app settings stored on the device

3. What Is Retained

  • Payment and subscription records are subject to a statutory retention duty. They are anonymized so that no individual can be identified, stored separately for the legally required period, and then destroyed.
  • EopuEopu does not collect or store payment method details; purchase histories are retained by each store (App Store / Google Play) under their own policies.
  • The nickname you used is released on deletion and becomes available to other users.
