1. Information We Collect

Users Who Are Not Signed In

  • Swimming Workout Data: Distance, time, lap data, heart rate, stroke style, calories, pool information (stored only on your device)

Signed-In Users (Free and Subscription Alike)

  • Account Information: Email, unique identifier (Apple or Google Sign-In)
  • Profile Information: Nickname, country code, time zone
  • Swimming Workout Data: The items above, synced to the cloud (free users up to a set number of sessions; subscribers without limit)

Subscription Users

  • Subscription Information: Subscription status, renewal date, transaction ID

Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage Analytics and Diagnostics: Feature usage events, crash reports, app version, device model, OS version (Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics)
  • Push Notification Token: A device identifier used to deliver notifications

2. Health Data (Apple Health & Health Connect)

EopuEopu reads your swimming records from Apple Health (HealthKit) on iOS and Health Connect on Android. Health data is handled only according to the principles below.

  • Read-only: The app only reads health data. It never writes to or modifies data in your health app.
  • What we read: Swimming workouts (distance, time, laps, stroke style), heart rate, active energy burned, and exercise route (open water swims)
  • How we use it: Solely to display and analyze your records inside the app and, when you are signed in, to calculate rankings.
  • No advertising, no sale: We never use health data for advertising or marketing, and we never sell it to third parties or provide it for data mining purposes.
  • Exercise route (GPS): Open water swim routes are stored only on your device and are never transmitted to our servers.
  • Withdrawing permission: You may revoke access at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Health, or in Android's Health Connect settings. Records already saved in the app remain after revocation; you can remove them by deleting the app or your account.

3. Purpose of Use

  • Social sign-in authentication and identity verification (Apple / Google)
  • Cloud synchronization of swimming records and restoration when changing devices
  • Providing community features such as rankings and levels
  • Subscription management and payment processing
  • Usage analysis for diagnosing errors and improving the service
  • Sending push notifications
  • Processing user feedback

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Retention Period

Data Type Retention Period
Local Storage Data Until app deletion
Cloud Swimming Data (Signed-In Users) Deleted without undue delay upon in-app account deletion
Authentication Account Information (Apple / Google) Deleted without undue delay upon in-app account deletion
Profile Information Deleted without undue delay upon in-app account deletion
Usage Analytics and Diagnostics Automatically deleted no later than 14 months after collection
Subscription & Payment Records Anonymized, then retained 5 years from contract termination (legal requirement)

Legal Retention Requirement

E-Commerce Act Article 6: Contract and withdrawal records for 5 years, payment records for 5 years

※ Payment and contract records must be retained for 5 years even after subscription cancellation for legal compliance.

Deletion Process

  • Local Data: Automatically erased by the device OS (iOS / Android) when the app is deleted
  • Cloud Data: Deleted without undue delay upon in-app account deletion (excluding data under legal retention duty)
  • Authentication Account: Deleted without undue delay upon account deletion
  • Payment & Subscription Records: Anonymized so individuals cannot be identified, retained for the statutory period, then destroyed
  • Method: Permanently deleted in a manner that prevents recovery of electronic files

5. Third-Party Data Sharing

Except where required by law, EopuEopu does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties (processing delegated for service operation is governed by Section 6). Health data is never used for advertising or marketing, and never sold.

Information Visible to Other Users

If you take part in the rankings, the following is shown to other users:

  • Nickname, country, swimmer class and level
  • Total distance and time for the period, average pace, and date of your last swim

Your email, account identifier, and individual workout details are never shown. You can stop appearing in the rankings by clearing your nickname in the app.
