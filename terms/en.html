1. Service Overview

EopuEopu is an app that records and analyzes your swimming workouts. Swimming records are read from Apple Health (iOS) or Health Connect (Android).

Available features are described in the app and on the store pages, and may be added or changed as the service improves. Some features require signing in or a paid subscription.

2. Subscription & Billing

Subscription Plans

  • Monthly subscription: Renews automatically every month
  • Annual subscription: Renews automatically every year

Auto-Renewal

Subscriptions renew automatically 24 hours before the current period ends and are charged to the store account you purchased through (Apple App Store or Google Play).

Cancellation

You can cancel in the store up to 24 hours before the next renewal. After cancellation, subscription features remain available until the end of the period you already paid for.

Manage subscription (iOS): Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions

Manage subscription (Android): Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions

3. Refund Policy

All payments and refunds are handled through the store you purchased from (Apple App Store or Google Play). Refund eligibility and procedures follow each store's policy.

  • App Store: Request through Apple's Report a Problem page
  • Google Play: Request according to the Google Play refund guide
